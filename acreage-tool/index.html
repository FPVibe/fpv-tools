    Help

    Add shape → click the map to drop it, then drag the white centre dot to move it and the coloured handle to resize. Area updates live.

    Polygon: click to add points, Enter or double-click to close, Backspace removes the last point, Esc cancels. Afterwards drag the dots to reshape.

    Squares and rectangles stay aligned north-up. Areas are geodesic (spherical earth), not measured on flat map degrees.
